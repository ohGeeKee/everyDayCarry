I. Introduction and Orientation

  • Opening Remarks: Reinforce that each team member represents the U.S. Army and is expected to maintain the highest standards of conduct, discipline, and appearance at all times.

  • Reference: TC 7-21.13, The Soldier’s Guide – emphasizes that every Soldier is responsible for maintaining Army Values, Warrior Ethos, and professional bearing both on and off duty.

  • Focus Areas: Loyalty, Duty, Respect, Selfless Service, Honor, Integrity, and Personal Courage.

  • Outcome: Ensure all personnel understand their role in contributing to the team’s mission and maintaining Army professionalism.

II. Baseline of Conduct and Responsibilities

  • Appearance and Discipline: AR 670-1, Wear and Appearance of Army Uniforms and Insignia – all Soldiers will maintain a neat, professional, and serviceable appearance at all times.

  • Physical Readiness: AR 600-9, The Army Body Composition Program – Soldiers must maintain physical readiness and appearance consistent with Army standards.

  • Training Readiness: AR 350-1, Army Training and Leader Development – mandates continuous individual and collective training to sustain readiness and proficiency.

  • Outcome: Reinforce that discipline and standards are non-negotiable components of unit readiness and effectiveness.

III. Adherence to Army Doctrine and Updated Policies

  • Doctrine Familiarization: TC 7-21.13 – outlines responsibilities of Soldiers as members of a profession grounded in trust and competence.

  • Leadership Development: TC 6-22.6, Army Team Building – promotes understanding of leadership dynamics and the principles of team development.

  • Training Compliance: AR 350-1 – emphasizes the importance of structured, progressive, and realistic training as a foundation for operational success.

  • Implementation: Conduct regular professional development sessions, ensure awareness of policy changes, and encourage use of the Army Publishing Directorate (APD) to access current doctrine.

IV. Team Cohesion and Resilience

  • Team Dynamics: Utilize TC 6-22.6 guidance to foster communication, trust, and collaboration.

  • Resilience Building: Encourage participation in Master Resilience Training (MRT) techniques; reinforce that resilience is critical to combat readiness and long-term well-being.

  • Inclusive Leadership: Recognize diversity and strengths within the team; promote respect and accountability.

  • Outcome: Develop a team identity based on mutual support, trust, and shared purpose.

V. Team Goals, Aspirations, and Timelines

  • Short-Term Goals: Complete required training per AR 350-1, maintain compliance with body composition standards (AR 600-9), and ensure uniform and appearance compliance (AR 670-1).

  • Mid-Term Goals: Enhance leadership capabilities through study and application of TC 6-22.6 and TC 7-21.13.

  • Long-Term Goals: Build a cohesive, resilient team capable of executing missions with competence, discipline, and adaptability.

  • Timeline:

  • Initial Review: Within 30 days – verify understanding and compliance.

  • Monthly Assessments: Evaluate training, readiness, and cohesion progress.

  • Ad Hoc Review: Conduct comprehensive assessment tied to unit objectives and individual professional development plans when necessary.
